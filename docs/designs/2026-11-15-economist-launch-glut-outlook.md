# Will the low-level material glut normalise, and how long to hold? — Economist (2026-11-15)

**Brief:** outlook, scenarios, holding periods, trim targets and thesis-ending signals for buy-and-hold positions in
low-level crafting materials; answers to the Strategist's eight questions. Inputs: the Researcher report
(`2026-11-15-leveling-materials-research.md`), the Strategist review (`2026-11-15-stockpile-positions-review.md`),
PLANNING, `tools/market/market.json` (US 38 hourly snapshots 11-14 00:17Z → 11-15 18:17Z; EU 17; one US flows day,
11-14), wago.tools `wowf` recipe tables. The Economist agent's first job; saved by the main session (ADR 0010). No profit is
promised; odds are judgements with their basis stated.

## Part 1: for the owner

### 0. What affects today's buy
1. **The plan holds: floor items plus Pyrite.** Changes of emphasis:
   - **Pyrite is the lead bet:** its recipe use is mostly at high skill (median ~230, mostly Blacksmithing 176–275 via
     Sulfuric Acid), where crafters are heading next; its supply comes from level ≤25 characters, which the population is leaving.
   - **Pristine Leather's demand is mostly now** (median recipe skill ~150): keep it small.
   - **Tigerseye, Shadowgem, Small Lustrous Pearl are "now" items** (median ~130): weakest gems to hold.
2. **Don't rush or sweep.** Floors refill within hours after buy-outs (113 Pristine Hide ~1,100 → 5,752 units in 4 h, back
   at vendor; Rosecap 114 pushed to 42× vendor, back to ~8× in 8 h). Buy only under the caps; check twice a day.
3. **Other stockpilers are already buying** (one-hour buy-outs of Pristine Hide, Jade, Rosecap, Iridescent Pearl). ~3,000–3,750
   Pristine Hide sell per day on big houses though almost no craftable recipe uses it yet: most floor buyers are speculators.
   The floor is real (sub-vendor listings get bought and vendored), but every rise will meet other holders selling: **sell in
   stages, starting early.**
4. **Buy priced items on the cheaper house.** Pyrite and Pristine Leather cost 1.3–2× more on 110/113 than 111/114, in both
   regions; looks structural (possibly PvP vs Normal worlds). Pyrite: 114 ~1.35s (under the 1.5s cap), 113 ~2.5s (above).
   You sell where you buy, so buy where your character lives; with characters on both, buy priced items on 114.
5. **Pyrite tranche 1 ≈ ⅓ of planned Pyrite.** The deposit change (US Tue 11-17, EU Wed 11-18) may bring cheaper small lots
   of 1-silver items for a few days.
6. **Not today:** Frilled Lichen / Cerulean Dye (no floor, 60–100× vendor); Rosecap (supply near peak; revisit December);
   Sulfuric Acid (hold Pyrite).

### 1. Bottom line
- Prices normalise **group by group**, not together. Level-gated scarce materials, led by Pyrite, are likeliest to rise:
  ~55% odds Pyrite on 114 trades ≥2× today (≥2.7s) by late January.
- Classic gems and Pristine Hide: floor-protected but crowded with large supply. Base case: within ~15% of vendor until
  ~12-09, then ~1.2–1.5× vendor by late January (~50–55%).
- The population is levelling fast: on day 11, Runecloth (level 45–60 zones) is posted almost as much as Linen. The window
  is **weeks, not months**.
- **Holding period:** to about late January, reviews at maintenance, end of November, raid week, mid-January; release floor
  bets that haven't moved by ~**2027-02-08**.
- **Confidence:** moderate on direction for Pyrite and Pristine Hide; low on timing and levels (38 h of prices, one day of sales).

**Selling:** floor volumes today are inflated by speculators; at 1.5–2× vendor expect real crafters only, ~25–50% of
today's volume. Slices ≤10–20% of a day's estimated sales, 2-hour listings, over 2–4 weeks per item, **starting at the
first target**. Rough absorption per big house over a 3-week sell-down: Pyrite ~1,000–3,000; Pristine Hide ~1,000–3,000
(once leatherworkers arrive); Jade ~1,000–2,000; Lesser Moonstone ~600–1,200; Iridescent Pearl ~400–1,000; Pristine Leather
~500–1,500. Until measured, keep the Strategist's rule: hold no more than is listed on your house now.

**Real return:** vendor floors are fixed in gold, so an unmoving floor item loses real value if gold inflates. Aim for
≥**+30% after costs** by late January, or the gold likely did better elsewhere (mount, training, flips).

**Cutting losses:** floor items have no stop (worst case = premium paid). Priced items: exit when the thesis breaks (check
B fails, scarce-supply hotfix, H-L2 refuted) or >30% under cost for a week while supply rises.

## Part 2: detail

### 5. Answers to the Strategist's questions
1. **"Normalised" by late January:** classic gems ~1.2–1.5× (Jade, Iridescent Pearl higher; weak gems ~1.0–1.2×), ~50%;
   tier 1 Pyrite 3–6s, Pristine Leather 4–7s, Lichen 25–45s, ~45–55%; tier 2 Pristine Hide 1.3–2×, Rosecap above ~9× only
   after supply peaks, ~50%. There is no pre-glut normal; the steady state is set by gathering alts and crafting demand.
2. **Inflation:** measured basket (284 items listed every hour, median ≥50c) on 113 changed 0.0% between first and last 12 h
   (114 −2.4%, 110 +0.8%, EU-113 −1.7%) — nothing measurable yet. Gold inflow grows as players reach 60 and raids open;
   mounts are big early sinks. H-E5: basket +15–40% by 01-31. Hurdle ≥+30% after costs.
3. **Tier-1 supply and an alt-farm ceiling:** supply is shrinking already (day 11 posted/day on 113: Runecloth ~51k, Linen
   ~71k, Silk ~74k, Thorium ~17k). Assumed share of active characters at ≤25: ~35–50% now, ~15–30% mid-December, floor
   ~10–20% (Legacy alts, new players). Pyrite supply rides on ore (Copper Ore 24–38× vendor), so responds little to its own
   price (H-E6). **Wage ceiling:** a level-25 miner alt at ~5–10 Pyrite/h plus ~1–2g ore vs a 60 earning ~5–15g/h (assumed)
   ⇒ alt farming pays from ~0.3–1g per Pyrite, far below 5g; for Pyrite the practical cap is the alt wage. Lichen and
   Pristine Leather are rarer, so their cap sits nearer 5g. Rests on unknown drop rates (check B, H-L2).
4. **Demand timing** (recipe units by skill band, wago `wowf`; recipes in the files, not actual crafting):

   | Item | Units | ≤125 | 126–175 | 176–225 | 226+ | Median |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Pyrite (direct + 2 per Acid) | 366 | 18% | 20% | 12% | 50% | ~230 |
   | Pristine Leather | 498 | 27% | 33% | 5% | 35% | ~150 |
   | Lichen (2 per Dye) | 876 | 26% | 31% | 20% | 24% | ~145 |
   | Pristine Hide | 277 | 0% | 0% | 20% | 80% | ~255 |
   | Rosecap (2 per Magenta Dye) | 606 | 0% | 4% | 46% | 50% | ~225 |

   Medians: Jade ~250, Iridescent Pearl ~255, Lesser Moonstone ~180, Shadowgem/Tigerseye/Small Lustrous Pearl ~130.
   Skill 200+ needs ~level 35+ (Era rule, assumed): the leading cohort is there now, the bulk mid-December to January.
   Raids 12-09: modest, recurring plus for necklace-enchant inputs (Acid/Pyrite, Cerulean Dye, Rosecap); negligible for
   gems and Hide; more gold. Hardcore: enclosed, probably its own AH (112/115/118 empty) — small negative for our houses.
5. **Ceilings:** the Black Market is a hard cap while fixed-price and unlimited (beta only; press reports of a "50g cap"
   intent; live unverified, check A). ~25–35% odds within 3 months of a change adding scarce supply (Forever is actively
   tuned: vendor cuts, deposit change, Black Lotus cap). Recipe families cap each gem near the cheapest substitute; not
   binding while all sit at the floor, but gems will move together (H-L5).
6. **Exit capacity and crowding:** floor volumes include speculators; H-E3 measures how much vanishes off the floor.
   Crowding caps rises early: sell into the first strength.
7. **Holding periods:** floor bets to late January, release review 02-08; Pyrite and Pristine Leather through the raid
   effect into January with priced exits. An unmoving floor bet costs only slots and real value.
8. **Mature-economy reference (P15):** valid as a rough range for classic gems (same items and vendor prices); invalid for
   scarce materials and population structure. Test once Forever has 2–3 months of data on unchanged items.

### 6. Mechanisms
**Who buys at vendor price.** Listing at 1.00× nets 0.95× after the cut — less than a vendor pays — so floor sellers don't
run the numbers or value convenience; their persistence (10–15k Pristine Hide posted per house per day) signals a real
glut. Buyers: (1) **vendor arbitrageurs**, taking only sub-vendor units (Pristine Hide below vendor in 8–10 of 38 hours on
113/114) and enforcing the floor; (2) **crafters**, real for Lesser Moonstone, Shadowgem, Tigerseye (skill 76–200 now),
small for Pristine Hide and much of Jade (recipes mostly 226+); (3) **speculators / would-be cornerers**, seen as one-hour
buy-outs (Pristine Hide 113 11-15 14:17 ~1,100 units, min to 2.75×; Jade 113 09:17 2.5×; Iridescent Pearl 114 11-14 13:17
10×; Rosecap both houses), each refilled in ~3–12 h. Implication: the floor is hard, floor supply is very elastic, rises
meet overhang, staged patient buying works (H-P2).

**Main session's quick read, checked:** Lichen/Dye rise on 113 confirmed but it is **one step** at 11-15 05:17 then held
13 h (other US +10–20%; EU-113 Lichen fell 31→26s). Rosecap 12→20s is a **decaying buy-out spike** (before it Rosecap fell
14×→8.4×). Sulfuric Acid 5.0→5.9s is a spike fully reversed. Pyrite 1.8→2.5s true on 113 only (114/111/110 flat; EU-113
3.9→3.0s). Pristine Leather **fell** on 113 (4.7→4.0s). Sales figures confirmed. Pyrite's `sold_est` is 0 on 114/110/111
because relists exceed the other terms — heavy cancel-and-repost competition, not zero sales (early vanishings ~700–1,100/day).

**Cross-house pattern** (× vendor, US / EU): Pyrite 113 2.5/3.0, 114 1.35/1.39, 110 1.74/2.0, 111 1.22/1.05; Lichen 113
104/88, 114 67/69, 110 106/92, 111 71/70. If 110/113 are PvP worlds, world PvP disrupting low-level gathering would explain
a structural gap (H-E2); supports the Researcher's house labels (#13).

### 9. Hypotheses for the Analyst (stated before looking)
- **H-E1 demand lull:** est. sales/day of front-loaded items (Pristine Leather, Shadowgem, Tigerseye, Small Lustrous Pearl)
  fall ≥30% from week of 11-16 to week of 12-14 while back-loaded (Pyrite, Jade, Pristine Hide) don't. Refuted if
  front-loaded fall no more.
- **H-E2 PvP premium:** weekly median min of tier-1 scarce on 110/113 ≥1.3× that on 111/114, both regions, through 12-31.
  Refuted if <1.15× for 2 weeks running.
- **H-E3 speculator share:** when a floor item stays >1.15× vendor for ≥24 h, est. sales fall ≥50% vs floor days. Refuted if <20%.
- **H-E4 buy-out reversion:** after a buy-out (min ≥2× within an hour and qty −50%), floor items return within 10% within
  12 h in ≥70% of cases; tier 1 in ≤50%. Refuted if floor items revert <50%.
- **H-E5 inflation:** broad basket (continuously listed, median ≥5s, not scarce) up ≥15% by 01-31 vs week of 11-16. Refuted if <5%.
- **H-E6 Pyrite supply follows ore:** daily Pyrite posted correlates >0.6 with Copper+Tin Ore posted, <0.2 with prior-week
  Pyrite price. Refuted if price correlation exceeds ore correlation.
- **H-E7 Rosecap supply peak:** 114 p25 lower on 12-07 than 11-16, then above it by 01-15. Refuted if it rises steadily from now.
