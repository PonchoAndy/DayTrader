DayTrader Mock-ups

The Designer turned the Strategist's intents into two tools and a shared visual language. Open each page; they work on a phone and in dark mode.

Sample data: prices and quantities are real (house 113, 14 Nov 2026, 03:17–18:17 UTC). Sale rates, depth, clearing prices, survival odds and the verdicts are invented to show the design, and are marked † or "sample". The controls don't change anything yet.

Deal scanner →"What's worth a look in my house right now?" Ranked by worse-case gold after fees, with certain and probable deals apart, and a section of traps that look like deals but aren't (Thorium Bar's reset, Crimson Lotus's falling price, a 1-silver bait). Item page: Thorium Bar →"Buy, post, wait or skip?" A verdict split by role (buyer: wait; holder: post), price and supply on one time line with the 17:17 reset marked, and all ten signals grouped by the question they answer. Visual language →The shared rules every screen follows: signal speed, data age, confidence, estimate versus firm, warning flags, and certain versus probable.
