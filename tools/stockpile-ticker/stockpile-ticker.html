    About these numbers

    Prices are gold with 4 decimals (0.0239 = 2 silver 39 copper). Each bar is the cheapest listing on house 114 in one hourly snapshot, for the last 14 days. Click a date under a chart to see that day hour by hour; the price scale then fits just that day. "Value now" is the lowest price × 0.95 (the 5% auction cut) × units held. "Pessimistic" and "Optimistic" use each item's cheapest listing at its worst and best hour of the last 24 hours, after the cut. "Realistic" uses the middle of those 24 hourly lowest prices. Pessimistic and Realistic never go below what a vendor pays, since you'd vendor instead.

    Numbers refresh every hour (about 15 minutes past). The Analyst writes a new briefing for each holding every 6 hours, and sooner if a lowest price moves 30% or more. Units sold per day comes from listing flows, built each night, and runs low: it counts only sales we can be sure of. GitHub skips about 3 hourly runs a day, so a few hours are missing. Briefings describe what the data shows; they aren't a forecast or a promise of profit.
