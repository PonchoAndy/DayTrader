    About these numbers

    Prices are gold with 4 decimals (0.0239 = 2 silver 39 copper). Each bar is the cheapest listing on house 114 at one snapshot: every 12 hours up to 11-19, then hourly for the last 24 hours. "Value now" is the lowest price × 0.95 (the 5% auction cut) × units held. "Pessimistic" and "Optimistic" use each item's cheapest listing at its worst and best hour of the last 24 hours, after the cut. Pessimistic never goes below what a vendor pays, since you'd vendor instead. Optimistic is mostly Pyrite's one-hour spike to 0.0515 on 11-19, so treat it as a ceiling seen once, not a target.

    Units sold per day comes from listing flows and runs low: it counts only sales we can be sure of. 11-19 isn't finished yet. Source: the market export of 2026-11-20 01:44 UTC, and your Stockpile Ledger (buys on 2026-11-15 22:31 UTC). This isn't a forecast or a promise of profit.
