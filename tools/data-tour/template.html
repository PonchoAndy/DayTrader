Trades with Blackwater

Blackwater, the neutral auction house, is open to both factions, so it's a second market you can always reach. These items cost less in one market than they sell for in the other, after the auction house's cut.

We don't know yet which houses share a world, so the other faction's house and the Blackwater house default to the ones of the same size; change them if you know better. Profit assumes a 5% cut at faction houses and 15% at Blackwater, which fan sites report but we haven't confirmed (#13). "Sells for" is the price a quarter of the way up that market's listings, so it's what you could realistically list at, not a lucky high.

Your faction's other houses

Show the gaps anyway

Which house are you in?

Each faction has five auction houses in the data (one always empty), and we don't yet know what separates them: different cities, different servers, or something else. You can tell us in two minutes in-game.

  1. At an auction house, search for one of the items below and note the cheapest price per unit.
  2. Do the same at an auction house in another city.
  3. Tell Claude the prices and where you were. Claude matches them to fresh data from that hour.

Prices shown are from 03:17 UTC and will have moved. They show the idea: each house has its own distinct price.

Source: Blizzard Game Data API (WoW Forever, US) hourly snapshots 23:17, 00:17, 02:17, 03:17 UTC; names from Blizzard's item databases and WoW Forever's game files. Floods and shortages compare 23:17 with 03:17, for items worth at least 5 g listed. Market size = units × typical price, capped at 3× the item's typical price across houses.
